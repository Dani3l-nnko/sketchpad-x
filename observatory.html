01THE IDEA

From scattered metrics
to a readable map.

This concept explores how an observability project could be presented: start with a simple topology, show what is being watched, then connect a useful alert to the system it describes.

The nodes and status messages are fictional demo labels. They are here to make the interaction tangible—not to imply a deployed environment or measured result.
