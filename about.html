01THE THROUGH-LINE

Make the hidden
understandable.

Technology can feel like a little magic when everything connects. The real craft is understanding what makes that possible.

This portfolio brings together the parts of IT I’m exploring: networking, cloud, security, automation, virtualization, and monitoring. They’re different disciplines, but they meet in the same place—building systems that are easier to see, reason about, and trust.

The stories here are meant to show the thinking behind the setup, not just the final screen. As real projects are added, this is where their context and lessons will live.
